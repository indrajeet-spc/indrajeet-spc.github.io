About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at S.P. College, Dumka, under Sido Kanhu Murmu University, Dumka, Jharkhand.

My academic work combines undergraduate and postgraduate physics teaching with an interest in scientific research and computational approaches to physical problems.

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes conceptual understanding, mathematical reasoning, physical intuition and connection between theory and experiment. I aim to make lecture materials structured, visual and useful for independent study.

Academic Interests

  • Physics education and undergraduate learning resources
  • Atmospheric Physics
  • Optics and light–matter interaction
  • Computational and simulation-based approaches to physics
