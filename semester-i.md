## Syllabus Topics

### Unit I: General Properties of Matter

Hooke's law, stress-strain diagram, elastic moduli and Poisson's ratio.

### Unit II: Central Force Motion and Oscillation

Central force motion, conservation of angular momentum, Kepler's laws, satellite motion and oscillations.

### Unit III: Special Theory of Relativity

Inertial and non-inertial frames, Michelson-Morley experiment, postulates of special relativity and Lorentz transformations.
