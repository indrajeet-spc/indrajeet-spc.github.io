## About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at **S.P. College, Dumka**, under Sido Kanhu Murmu University, Dumka, Jharkhand.

My academic work combines undergraduate and postgraduate physics teaching with an interest in scientific research and computational approaches to physical problems.

## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes:

- Conceptual understanding
- Mathematical reasoning
- Physical intuition
- Connection between theory and experiment

## Academic Interests

- **Physics Education** — Development of effective undergraduate learning resources
- **Atmospheric Physics** — Study of atmospheric processes and physical phenomena
- **Optics** — Light, optical phenomena, and light–matter interaction
- **Computational Physics** — Computational and simulation-based approaches to physics
