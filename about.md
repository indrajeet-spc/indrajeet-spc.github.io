## About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Physics at **S.P. College, Dumka**, under Sido Kanhu Murmu University, Dumka, Jharkhand.

My academic work combines undergraduate and postgraduate physics teaching with an interest in scientific research and computational approaches to physical problems.

## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching approach emphasizes:

- Conceptual understanding
- Mathematical reasoning
- Physical intuition
- Connection between theory and experiment

## Research Experience

- Designed and operated experimental setups for:
	- Fabrication of carbon thin films using pulsed laser deposition.
	- Synthesis of nanodiamonds using pulsed laser ablation in liquid.
	- Plasma diagnostics of graphite plasma.
	- Nonlinear optical studies of carbon thin films.
	- Laser beam micromachining.
	- Optical fiber sensing characterization.
- Performed structural and optical characterization of diamond-like carbon, graphitic thin films, nanodiamonds and graphene using:
	- Raman spectroscopy.
	- UV-Vis-NIR and FTIR spectroscopy.
	- Spectroscopic ellipsometry.
	- Scanning electron microscopy.
	- Transmission electron microscopy.
	- Atomic force microscopy.

## Technical Skills

- Operated sophisticated scientific instruments, including:
	- Raman spectrometer.
	- Transmission electron microscope.
	- UV-Vis-NIR spectrometer.
	- Monochromator.
	- Optical spectrum analyzer.
	- High-vacuum chamber.
- Handled Q-switched Nd:YAG, continuous-wave He-Ne and continuous-wave Ar-ion lasers.
